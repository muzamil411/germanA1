🔠 Alphabet Aur Awaazein

German ke harf bilkul English jaise hain — magar bolne ka tareeqa alag hai. Yehi chapter aap ki buniyad (foundation) hai.

💡 Yaad Rakho German mein jo likha hota hai, wohi bola jata hai — English ki tarah "silent letters" ka jhamela nahi! Aik dafa awaazein seekh lein, phir aap har naya lafz khud sahi parh sakenge. 🎉 (German spelling is phonetic — you read exactly what is written.)

⚠️ Galti Se Bacho — Ye 3 Harf Sab Ko Dhoka Dete Hain
  • W ko English ka "w" na samjhein — ye "v" bolta hai: Wasser = "vasser" ✅
  • V zyada tar "f" bolta hai: Vater = "faater" ✅
  • Z hamesha "ts" bolta hai: Zeit = "tsait" ✅ (na ke "zeit")

buchstabieren (bookh-shta-beeren) = "spell karna". Ye A1 speaking exam ke pehle hisse mein zaroor poocha jata hai — aur Germany mein har jagah (bank, doctor, Amt) kaam aata hai.

💡 Spell Karne Ke Kaam Ke Lafz
  • doppel (doppel) = double. Anna = "A – Doppel-N – A" 👉 do aik jaise harf ho to.
  • groß (gross) = capital, klein (klain) = small. großes A = capital A.

🔤 ß — Eszett Ka Raaz

ß koi "B" nahi hai! Ye do "s" ke barabar hai — hamesha "ss" boliye. Iska naam Eszett (ess-tsett) ya scharfes S hai. (ß is simply a sharp "ss" — never a B.)

💡 Keyboard Par ß Na Ho To? Ghabrayein nahi — ss likh dein, ghalat nahi hoga: Straße = Strasse ✅. Switzerland mein to log hamesha ss hi likhte hain. 🏔️ ß hamesha chhota likha jata hai, capital letters mein SS ban jata hai.
