🌍 German Kahan Boli Jati Hai?

German (apni zaban mein Deutsch, "doytsh") taqreeban 13 crore logon ki maadari zaban hai — Europe mein sab se zyada boli jane wali zaban. In mulkon ke ilawa Liechtenstein, Luxembourg aur Belgium ke kuch hisson mein bhi German chalti hai.

🔍 German English Se 4 Cheezon Mein Alag Hai
  1. Har ism (noun) capital letter se shuru hota hai — beech jumle mein bhi: der Tisch, das Kind.
  2. Har ism ke 3 mein se aik khaana hota hai — derM / dieF / dasN. English mein sirf "the" hai, German mein teen. 😅
  3. Verb ki jagah muqarrar hai — seedhe jumle mein hamesha doosri jagah par (Chapter 6).
  4. Cases — lafz ka roop uske kaam ke mutabiq badalta hai, bilkul Urdu ke "ne / ko / se" ki tarah (Chapter 7).
💡 Yaad Rakho Ye 4 cheezein pehle hafte mein mushkil lagti hain aur teesre hafte mein bilkul normal. Har seekhne wala isi raste se guzarta hai — is liye himmat na harein. 💪 (Everyone finds these hard in week one and normal by week three.)
