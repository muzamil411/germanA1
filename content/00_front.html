📖 Is Kitab Ko Kaise Parhein?

Sirf 5 asaan usool — inhein maan lein, German zaban khud-ba-khud aa jayegi.

🪜 5 Sunehray Usool (5 Golden Rules)
  1. Har roz sirf 2 safhe. Zyada nahi. 50 din mein poori kitab khatam — aur sab yaad bhi rahega. (Just 2 pages a day.)
  2. Har German lafz bol kar parhein. Har lafz ke saath talaffuz (pronunciation) Roman Urdu mein likha hai — chup chaap parhne se zaban nahi aati. 🗣️
  3. Exercise chhorna mana hai. Har chapter ke aakhir mein 🎯 Mini Test hai. Pehle khud karein, phir safha 99 par jawabat (answers) check karein.
  4. Har naye lafz ke saath uska article yaad karein. Sirf Tür nahi — die TürF. Ye aadat shuru mein hi daal lein. ⚠️
  5. Hafte mein aik din sirf dohrayein (revision) — naya kuch na parhein. 🔁
🏷️ Kitab Ke Nishaan (Symbols Used in This Book)

German ke har ism (noun) ke saath aik "article" aata hai. Ye kitab har lafz ke saath aik chhota nishaan deti hai, taake aap ko foran pata chale ke lafz kis khaane (gender) ka hai — ye nishaan black & white print par bhi bilkul saaf nazar aata hai:

NishaanArticleKya MatlabMisaal · Example
M Mder Maskuline — mardana (masculine) der Vater — abbu father
F Fdie Feminine — zanana (feminine) die Mutter — ammi mother
N Ndas Neutrum — darmiyani (neuter) das Kind — bacha child
