🔠 Alphabet Aur Awaazein

German ke harf bilkul English jaise hain — magar bolne ka tareeqa alag hai. Yehi chapter aap ki buniyad hai.

💡 Yaad Rakho German mein jo likha hota hai, wohi bola jata hai — English ki tarah "silent letters" ka jhamela nahi! Aik dafa awaazein seekh lein, phir aap har naya lafz khud sahi parh sakenge. 🎉

⚠️ Galti Se Bacho — Ye 3 Harf Sab Ko Dhoka Dete Hain
  • W ko English ka "w" na samjhein — ye "v" bolta hai. Wasser = "vasser" ✅ (na ke "wasser")
  • V zyada tar "f" bolta hai. Vater = "faater" ✅
  • Z hamesha "ts" bolta hai. Zeit = "tsait" ✅ (na ke "zeit")
🎩 Umlaut — Do Nuqton Ka Jaadu (ä ö ü)

Ye German ke apne khaas 3 harf hain. Do nuqte upar lagte hain aur awaaz badal jati hai. Bolne ka asaan tareeqa niche diya hai:

HarfKaise Bolein (asaan tareeqa)Misaal
ä (a-Umlaut)Bilkul Urdu ke "ay" jaisa — jaise "hai" mein. Munh khula rakhein.Äpfel (epfel) — seb
ö (o-Umlaut)Honth "o" ki tarah gol karein, magar bolein "ay". 👄schön (shoen) — khoobsurat
ü (u-Umlaut)Honth "oo" ki tarah gol karein, magar bolein "ee". 👄Tür (tuer) — darwaza
💡 Yaad Rakho — ß (Eszett) ß koi B nahi hai! Ye do "s" ke barabar hai — hamesha "ss" boliye. Straße = "shtraasse" (sarak). Agar keyboard par ß na ho to ss likh dein, ghalat nahi hoga. Switzerland mein to log hamesha ss hi likhte hain. 🏔️
